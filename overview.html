Overview

Project Overview

StudyVerse is an educational website that helps students in Bahrain plan their studies, stay focused and find places to study.

Website title
StudyVerse
Category
Educational website
Target audience
School, college and university students in Bahrain who want to plan their studies, improve their focus and find good places to study.

Problem statement

Students often struggle to organise their study time, stay focused and find a suitable place to study. These tools are usually spread across different apps and websites. StudyVerse brings them together in one friendly place, with study spots that are relevant to students in Bahrain.

Project objectives

  • Help students plan and organise their study time.
  • Support focus and wellbeing with simple tools.
  • Help students discover study spots in Bahrain.
  • Provide a responsive website for desktop, tablet and mobile, in light and dark mode.
  • Use AI tools responsibly and document how they were used.

Requirement Analysis

What the website must do, and how well it must do it.

Functional requirements

  • FR1User registration: a form on the Home page collects first name, last name, mobile number and email.
  • FR2Form validation: names need at least 3 characters, the mobile number exactly 8 digits and the email a valid format, with clear success and error messages.
  • FR3Navigation system: one shared navigation bar links all seven pages.
  • FR4Login demo: a login window shows a welcome message (front-end only).
  • FR5Theme switch: a moon/sun button changes between light and dark mode.
  • FR6Study planner: tools to plan study tasks.
  • FR7Focus tools: Pomodoro timer, Mood Matcher, Stress SOS and Break Generator.
  • FR8Study spot finder: study spots in Bahrain that can be filtered by location, budget, noise level, Wi-Fi and power sockets.

Non-functional requirements

  • NFR1Performance: lightweight pages that load quickly.
  • NFR2Accessibility: semantic HTML, labelled form fields, alt text on images and readable colours in both themes.
  • NFR3Security: user input is validated, and the demo collects no passwords.
  • NFR4Usability: a simple layout, consistent cards and buttons, and the same navigation on every page.
  • NFR5Responsiveness: layouts adapt to desktop, tablet and mobile.
  • NFR6Maintainability: shared styles and files keep all pages consistent and easy to update.
